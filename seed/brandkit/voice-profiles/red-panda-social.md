# Voice: Red Panda Social

Who's speaking: the Red Panda social team. They sound like the funniest person in your bike lane: a local who's been rained on more times than you and finds it hilarious. They write to followers as friends and fellow riders, never as customers.
Signature move: deadpan observations about everyday Dutch riding (the wind, the rain, tram tracks, the ferry, apple cake) that end with a small practical nudge or a wink to the product. Never a hard sell.

Tone: playful | warm | deadpan | quick   (max 4)

Sentence style:
- Length: short. Punchy fragments are fine. One idea per post.
- Point of view: we (Red Panda) and you (the rider).
- Contractions: yes, always.
- Phrasing: direct and conversational ("We tested it. In a storm. On purpose.").

Words:
- Use: headwind, fietspad, apple cake, ferry, overshoes, ding, legend, honestly
- Avoid: game-changer, unleash, epic, revolutionary, crush it, hack, "cyclists", "Buy now!"

Formatting:
- Sentence case. No ALL CAPS except one word, very occasionally, for comic effect.
- At most two emoji per post, used as punchlines, not decoration: 🚲 🌧️ 💨 🍰 ⛴️
- At most two hashtags, at the end, only when useful (#fietsen #Amsterdam).
- One Dutch word per post at most, and make it obvious from context.
- British English, metric units, € before prices. Never invent specs or prices.
- Jokes punch at the weather, the wind and ourselves, never at drivers, tourists, pedestrians or other brands.
- Keep it safe: no phones in hand while riding, lights on after dark, two locks.

Platforms:
- Instagram and TikTok captions: a hook line, one or two short lines, optional CTA.
- X and Threads: one line, two at most.
- LinkedIn: the same humour, one notch calmer, a clearer point.

Examples:
- "Forecast: 'light drizzle'. Amsterdam: hold my apple cake. 🌧️🍰 Overshoes first, trousers second. Trust us."
- "The wind on the Afsluitdijk has opinions. Our E-Bikes have counter-arguments. 💨"
- "PSA: tram tracks are exactly one tyre wide. That's not a coincidence, that's a warning. Cross at an angle."
- "Two locks. One to something fixed. Not your mate's bike. Something FIXED."
- "Free ferry, 5 minutes, zero effort. Amsterdam Noord is basically a teleport with seagulls. ⛴️"

Sound check:
✓ "Headwind all the way to Zandvoort? Tailwind all the way home. That's the deal. 💨"
✗ "Experience the revolutionary power of our game-changing e-bikes — unleash your epic ride today!"
