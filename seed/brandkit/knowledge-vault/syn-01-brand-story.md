# Red Panda Bicycles: who we are

Red Panda Bicycles is an Amsterdam bicycle brand. We design city bikes, e-bikes, lifestyle bikes and riding accessories for people who ride every day, in every kind of Dutch weather, and we look after them in our own Amsterdam workshop.

## Where we come from
Red Panda started as a small repair workshop in Amsterdam, fixing other people's bikes long before we built our own. That history still shapes everything: we design bikes a mechanic would be happy to work on, and we keep parts available long after a model leaves the catalogue. Our workshop lead, Pieter Bakker, still says the best design review is watching a bike come back for its third service.

## Why "Red Panda"
Red pandas are small, curious, happiest outdoors and not remotely in a hurry. They are also red, like the asphalt of a Dutch fietspad. It felt right.

## What we believe
- **Everyday over extreme.** The bicycle is transport first. We build for the school run, the commute, the Sunday ride to Durgerdam and the trip to the shops, not for podiums.
- **Built to be kept.** A good bike should last a decade or more. We favour serviceable parts, honest materials and repair over replacement.
- **Honest about what bikes can do.** We talk about real-world range, real weather and real maintenance. We would rather under-promise and have a rider pleasantly surprised on a headwind day.
- **Ride with the weather, not against it.** Rain, wind and dark winter evenings are part of riding in the Netherlands. We design and write for them rather than pretending they don't exist.
- **An open workshop door.** Riders are welcome to come in, ask questions, watch a repair and borrow a pump. Community is not a campaign; it's who walks through the door.

## What we make
Our range is organised the way the website is: **City Bikes** (upright, practical, made for hopping across town), **E-Bikes** (effortless miles whatever the distance, from commuters to cargo bikes), **Lifestyle Bikes** (trail, enduro and hardtail bikes for riding for the fun of it) and **Accessories** (bags, locks, lights and helmets).

## Amsterdam BC
Amsterdam BC is the Red Panda riders' club. Members sign in on the Red Panda website with their member account and carry a member number in the format RP-000000. Members get early access to new models and member-only offers, and they are the first people we talk to when we design something new.

## The people you'll hear from
Our stories are written by the people who ride and fix the bikes: guides and tips from long-time rider Sanne de Vries, workshop diaries from Pieter Bakker, and the occasional overheard remark from Anna on the front desk.
