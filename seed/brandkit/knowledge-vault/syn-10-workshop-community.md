# The Red Panda workshop and community

## The Amsterdam workshop
The Red Panda workshop is the heart of the brand: part repair shop, part showroom, part neighbourhood living room. It is run by workshop lead Pieter Bakker. Anna on the front desk will tell you, kindly and accurately, how you look in your rain gear.

What happens there:
- Repairs and servicing for Red Panda bikes, and for other brands too. A puncture is a puncture.
- Seasonal checks: a spring service after winter, and a pre-winter check of lights, brakes, tyres and battery before the dark months.
- E-bike diagnostics and battery health checks.
- Test rides on City Bikes, E-Bikes and Lifestyle Bikes, so you can feel the difference before choosing.
- Advice, free. Ask about routes, locks, rain gear or how to get a child seat on a bike.

## The Sunday social ride
Most Sundays a group ride leaves from the workshop at 10:00. It's a relaxed, no-drop ride of 30–40 km out into Waterland or along the Amstel, with a coffee-and-apple-cake stop in Durgerdam, Monnickendam or Ouderkerk aan de Amstel. Every kind of bike is welcome, and nobody gets left behind.

## Amsterdam BC
Amsterdam BC is the Red Panda riders' club. Members sign in on the Red Panda website, get early access to new models and member-only offers, and get first invitations to rides and workshop evenings. Long-time members helped shape our commuter e-bikes, and we keep asking them what we should build next.

## How we talk to our community
- Like neighbours, not like a brand. We use first names and speak plainly.
- We listen, especially when something isn't working. A rider who tells us about a problem is doing us a favour.
- We celebrate everyday riding: the commute, the school run and the rainy lunch ride, not only the big adventures.
- We share the stories of our riders and our workshop team, in their own words.
