# The riders we serve

Red Panda's main rider groups, for tailoring tone and topics. These are broad descriptions for writing, not individual customers.

## Daily e-bike commuters
They ride 10–25 km each way into Amsterdam from towns such as Almere, Zaandam, Haarlem, Amstelveen and Purmerend, or across the city from Noord, Oost or Nieuw-West. Many have owned a Red Panda e-bike for years, including the RP Volt. They value reliability and real-world range above all else, then comfort, weather protection and easy servicing. They respond to honesty, practical detail and respect for their experience. They do not respond to hype.
Topics: range in winter, battery care, commuting kit, servicing, new commuter models such as the RP Roam.

## City riders
They live in or near the centre and ride short trips daily: work, shops, friends, the park. They want a bike that's comfortable, good-looking, easy to park and hard to steal.
Topics: City Bikes such as The Jordaan and the Heritage Step-Through, locks, lights, the unwritten rules of Amsterdam riding.

## Weekend explorers and day-trippers
They ride for pleasure: day trips out of Amsterdam, the coast, Waterland, the Veluwe. Some ride E-Bikes to go further; some ride Lifestyle Bikes on trails.
Topics: day trips, routes beyond Amsterdam, the Trip Planner, saddlebags and frame bags, seasonal rides.

## Families
Parents carrying children, shopping and everything else. Safety, stability and practicality matter most.
Topics: the Cargo Cub E-Cargo Bike, child seats, school runs, riding in the rain with small passengers.

## Newcomers, expats and visitors
New to riding in the Netherlands and slightly terrified of tram tracks. They need friendly, practical guidance without being made to feel silly.
Topics: "5 Things Nobody Tells You About Riding in Amsterdam", rules and etiquette, Dutch cycling words, choosing a first bike.
