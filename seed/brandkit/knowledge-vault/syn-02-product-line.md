# Red Panda product line overview

A guide to the Red Panda range: what each bike and accessory is for, and who it suits. For specifications, battery capacities, range figures and prices, always refer to the live product page. Never invent specs or prices in content.

## E-Bikes
- **RP Roam**: our next-generation commuter e-bike, currently available to pre-order. Roam was designed around what daily riders told us matters most: reliability and real-world range. It is built for long, everyday commutes into the city in all weathers.
- **RP Volt**: Red Panda's first commuter e-bike, sold from 2021 to mid-2024. Thousands of Volts still carry their owners into Amsterdam every day, and Volt owners are some of our most loyal riders. We continue to service and support the Volt in our workshop.
- **Glide E-Commuter**: a lighter, sleeker e-commuter for riders who want assistance without the bulk.
- **Cargo Cub E-Cargo Bike**: an electric bakfiets for children, groceries, dogs and the occasional Christmas tree.
- **Volt Trail E-MTB**: an electric mountain bike for forest trails, dunes and the hills of Limburg.

## City Bikes
- **The Jordaan**: classic Dutch style made for everyday city life. An upright, comfortable ride with timeless details for commutes, coffee runs and wherever the day takes you.
- **Red Panda Heritage Step-Through**: an easy step-through frame for relaxed, upright riding in everyday clothes.
- **Red Panda Metro Hybrid**: a quicker, lighter city bike for riders who like a bit more pace on the way to work.

## Lifestyle Bikes
- **Red Panda Bamboo Trail 140 Full Suspension**: a full-suspension trail bike for rougher, rootier riding.
- **Red Panda Enduro X Carbon**: a carbon enduro bike for riders chasing descents.
- **Red Panda Ridgeline 29 Hardtail**: a versatile 29-inch hardtail for trails and gravel.

## Accessories
- **Touring Saddlebag**: a roomy saddlebag for day trips and touring.
- **Red Panda Commuter Pannier 20L**: a waterproof pannier sized for a laptop, lunch and rain gear.
- **Red Panda Explorer Frame Bag**: a frame bag for snacks, tools and a phone within reach.
- **Red Panda Fortress U-Lock**: a heavy-duty lock for locking to something fixed (always use it alongside your frame lock).
- **Red Panda Beacon 1200 Front Light** and **Red Panda Tail Glow Rear Light**: bright, rechargeable lights for dark winter commutes.
- **Red Panda Aero Road Helmet** and **Red Panda Trail Guard MTB Helmet**: helmets for faster road riding and trails. Speed pedelec riders are legally required to wear an approved helmet.

## Choosing a bike, in one line
Under about 5 km, a City Bike is perfect for hopping across town. Beyond that, or when the wind is up, an E-Bike makes the miles effortless. That is the same rule our Trip Planner uses.
