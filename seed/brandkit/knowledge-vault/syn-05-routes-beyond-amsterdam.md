# Great rides beyond Amsterdam

A Red Panda shortlist of rides elsewhere in the Netherlands, for day trips and weekend tours. Combine them with the train. Most routes can be planned on the knooppunten (junction) network.

## Hoge Veluwe National Park (Gelderland)
Heathland, forest and drifting sand dunes. Entry to the park includes free use of its famous white bikes (witte fietsen), or bring your own. The Kröller-Müller Museum, with one of the world's largest Van Gogh collections, sits in the middle of the park, and its sculpture garden is best reached by bike. Tip: the wider Veluwe also has marked mountain-bike trails, which is Volt Trail E-MTB and Ridgeline 29 territory.

## The Afsluitdijk (North Holland to Friesland)
A dead-straight, 32 km dike separating the Wadden Sea from the IJsselmeer, with a cycle path along its length. On one side the sea, on the other a lake the size of a small country, and nothing at all to stop the wind. Check the forecast and pick your direction: with a tailwind it is exhilarating; into a headwind it is a character-building exercise best done on an E-Bike.

## The Bollenstreek bulb fields (between Haarlem and Leiden)
From mid-March to mid-May, and usually peaking in April, the fields between Haarlem and Leiden turn into stripes of tulips, hyacinths and daffodils. Keukenhof is the famous garden, but the fields themselves are free to ride past. Go on a weekday and stay on the paths: the fields are farms, not photo studios.

## Kinderdijk (near Rotterdam)
Nineteen 18th-century windmills along the polder waterways, a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Ride out from Rotterdam along the river Lek, or take the waterbus with your bike and ride back.

## Texel (Wadden Islands)
Take the ferry from Den Helder with your bike. Texel has beaches, dunes, sheep, a red lighthouse at the northern tip and villages made for coffee stops. It's big enough for a full day and small enough that you can't really get lost.

## The Kromme Rijn (Utrecht)
From Utrecht, follow the winding Kromme Rijn river south-east past orchards, country estates and castles towards Wijk bij Duurstede. It's gentle, green and ideal for a first longer ride.

## Zuid-Limburg
Proof that the Netherlands is not entirely flat. The rolling hills around Valkenburg include the Cauberg, a famous climb in Dutch road racing. Expect views, vineyards and legs that will remember the day.

## Taking your bike on the train
- Buy a bike day ticket (dagkaart fiets) for your bike alongside your own ticket.
- Bikes are not allowed on trains during weekday rush hours (roughly 06:30–09:00 and 16:30–18:00). July and August are exempt.
- Folding bikes travel free when folded. Cargo bikes and tandems generally can't go on the train.
- Use the carriages marked with a bicycle symbol, and let people off first.
