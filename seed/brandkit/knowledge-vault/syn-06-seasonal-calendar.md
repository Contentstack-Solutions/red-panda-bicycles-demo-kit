# The Dutch riding year: a seasonal content calendar

What riding in the Netherlands feels like month by month, with the moments Red Panda content can build on.

## January–February: the dark, crisp months
- Short days: in Amsterdam the sun sets around 16:30 in early January.
- Main cycle routes are often gritted before frosty nights, but side streets and bridges freeze first. Black ice (gladheid) is the real risk.
- Lights on, always. Bring e-bike batteries indoors overnight and charge them at room temperature.
- Content moments: winter riding kit, lights, battery care in the cold, the smugness of riding past a traffic jam at -2°C.

## March–May: spring, bulbs and the first long rides
- The bulb fields bloom from mid-March to mid-May, peaking in April.
- Clocks go forward at the end of March, and suddenly there's light after work.
- King's Day on 27 April: the city fills with orange and street markets. Leave the bike at home in the centre; it's too busy.
- Spring winds are strong and changeable. Plan day trips with the wind at your back on the way home.
- Content moments: spring service check, first day trips, tulip rides, pumping up tyres that have been sad all winter.

## June–August: long evenings and beach rides
- Around midsummer it stays light in Amsterdam until after 22:00.
- On sunny days the sea breeze often picks up in the afternoon, so the ride back from Zandvoort can be into the wind.
- Holiday touring season: panniers, saddlebags, ferries and campsites.
- Content moments: evening rides, beach rides, touring, water, sunscreen, and waiting for a crowded ferry to Noord.

## September–October: harvest light and autumn storms
- Back-to-school and back-to-work commuting peak.
- The first autumn storms arrive, and wet leaves on cycle paths are slippery.
- Clocks go back at the end of October, and commutes home turn dark overnight.
- Content moments: rain gear rituals ("overshoes first"), pre-winter workshop checks, lights, the joy of a rainy ride.

## November–December: gezellig season
- Darkest time of year: in December the sun sets around 16:30.
- Sinterklaas on 5 December, then Christmas. Cargo bikes carrying trees are a seasonal sight.
- Content moments: gift guides (lights, locks, panniers), reflective kit, riding to Christmas markets, being the only person smiling in the drizzle.
